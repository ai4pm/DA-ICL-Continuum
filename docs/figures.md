# Manuscript figure catalogue

The seven panels below are byte-for-byte copies of Figures 1--4 referenced by the camera-ready manuscript. They are canonical visual references, not outputs that the repository silently overwrites. Verify them with:

```bash
cd figures/manuscript
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
```

Newly generated synthetic figures are written beneath `results/<experiment>/figures/`.

## Figure 1: framework

Original caption: “Distribution-aware ICL for the ancestry-continuum modeling.”

| Panel | File | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | [`framework/ICL_continuum.png`](../figures/manuscript/framework/ICL_continuum.png) | Illustrates query-specific ancestry-continuum ICL. Genetic coordinates locate a query, local genetically close development patients form a context, and their omics features/outcomes condition the prediction. | 1b | [`framework/DACE_Illustration.png`](../figures/manuscript/framework/DACE_Illustration.png) | Illustrates DACE. ## Synthetic performance figures

### Figure 2: rotation and offset

Original caption: “AUROCs of ancestry-discrete, oracle ancestry-continuum, and empirical ancestry-continuum modeling on synthetic data with increasing offset degree *o* and rotation degree *r*, respectively. Setting both *o* and *r* to 0 indicates that the relationship between *y* and **x** does not vary across **s**.”

| Panel | File | X axis / interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 2a | [`synthetic/auroc_vs_offset_degree_rotation0.png`](../figures/manuscript/synthetic/auroc_vs_offset_degree_rotation0.png) | Offset sweep at zero rotation. Higher AUROC is better. |
| 2b | [`synthetic/auroc_vs_rotation_degree_offset0.png`](../figures/manuscript/synthetic/auroc_vs_rotation_degree_offset0.png) | Rotation sweep at zero offset. Higher AUROC is better. |

Methods are nearest-centroid discrete logistic, query-specific empirical KNN100 logistic, and oracle local logistic. Inputs are generated by `src/bibm_replication/synthetic.py`. Reproduce aggregate tables and fresh figures with the commands in `docs/synthetic.md`; sources are `metrics_by_replicate.csv` and `summary_metrics.csv`.

## TCGA performance figures

### Figure 4: 18-task AUROC distributions

Original caption: “Run-level AUROC distributions of DA-ICL for ancestry-continuum modeling and conventional tabular methods for ancestry-discrete modeling across 18 TCGA tasks, evaluated on AFR test samples. The center line denotes the median, box edges indicate the first and third quartiles, whiskers indicate the non-outlier range, and individual points represent outliers.”

- File: [`tcga/hparam_afr_min_core_methods_tcga_budget30s_da_icl_auroc_boxplot.png`](../figures/manuscript/tcga/hparam_afr_min_core_methods_tcga_budget30s_da_icl_auroc_boxplot.png)
- Methods: DA-ICL with continuum KNN100+DACE, and ancestry-discrete elastic net, random forest, and XGBoost.
- Population/tasks: AFR test patients, 18 combinations of BRCA/KIRC/UCEC, OS/PFI, and mRNA/microRNA/methylation.
- Metric: higher AUROC is better; points summarize split/run-level task performance, not individual patients.
